Welcome to Parikshit

We are a student satellite team from Manipal Institute of Technology, India — building a 2U-class nanosatellite designed and developed entirely in-house. Since 2013, Parikshit has been the platform for young minds to explore, innovate, and create for space.

Our Subsystems

🌍

ADCS

Determines and controls the satellite’s orientation in orbit with precision and stability.

📡

Comms

Handles communication between the satellite and ground station for data exchange.

🔋

EPS

Manages power generation, storage, and distribution for all satellite systems.

📟

ODHS

The satellite’s onboard brain — processes commands and manages subsystem interactions.

🛰️

Payload

Houses the mission’s main instruments and sensors for scientific and educational goals.

⚙️

STMS

Responsible for structural strength and thermal balance under space conditions.
